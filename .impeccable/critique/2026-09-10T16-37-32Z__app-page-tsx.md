### Priority Issues

#### [P1] Missing Abort/Cancel Action During Multi-Persona Synthesis
- **Why it matters**: A complete 4-persona reasoning cycle takes 10–20 seconds. If a user submits a typo, wrong constraint, or changes their mind, they cannot cancel without reloading the page and losing their inputs.
- **Fix**: Add an "Abort Analysis" button to the active synthesis banner that triggers an `AbortController.abort()` signal.
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable harden`

#### [P1] Intake Panel Visual Density & Cognitive Load (8+ Competing Controls)
- **Why it matters**: The intake view places Narrative textarea, Objectives input, Constraints input, Security Passcode, Voice Mic, Initiate Synthesis, Case Dossier Drawer button, and Telemetry Badges all in the initial screen, violating the ≤4 items working memory guideline.
- **Fix**: Visually subordinate the passcode and secondary badges into a sleek utility bar or modal clearance check, letting Dilemma & Objectives dominate the primary focal path.
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable layout`

#### [P2] Mechanical Tell: Side-Tab Accent Border
- **Why it matters**: `components/VoiceReadbackController.tsx` uses `borderLeft: "3px solid ..."`, flagged by the detector as an AI-generated UI trope.
- **Fix**: Replace the one-sided border with a full hairline border (`1px solid var(--border-medium)`) and subtle cyan glow.
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable polish`

#### [P2] Lack of Keyboard Accelerators
- **Why it matters**: Power users and executive decision-makers need rapid keyboard-driven interaction without having to reach for the mouse.
- **Fix**: Bind `Cmd/Ctrl + Enter` to initiate synthesis, `Esc` to close open drawers and outcome modals, and `/` or `Cmd+K` to focus the dilemma intake.
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable adapt`

### Persona Red Flags

- **Alex (Impatient Power User)**: Cannot press `Ctrl+Enter` to submit. Must manually click the "INITIATE O.D.I.N. SYNTHESIS" button with the mouse. Cannot press `Esc` to close the Case Dossier Drawer.
- **Jordan (Confused First-Timer)**: Overwhelmed by engineering jargon in status chips (`MRL 768-dim`, `AES-256-GCM`, `RLS: ACTIVE`). Unsure whether "Known Constraints" are required or optional, and unsure how to format objectives for best results.
- **Morgan (Skeptical Executive)**: Wants to immediately see the bottom line recommendation. Persona tabs require manual clicking through Quant, Strategist, and Behaviorist before reaching The Judge's definitive verdict.

### Minor Observations
- The masked passcode input lacks a "Show/Hide" toggle button for users who want to verify their passphrase before hitting submit.
- The outcome status badges in the Case History Drawer look crisp, but could include quick tooltips explaining what "Deviated" vs "Superseded" means in the context of the feedback loop.

### Questions to Consider

- Could the Security Clearance Passcode be remembered in session storage so executive users don't have to re-enter it across reloads?
- Would an inline example toggle ("Load sample dilemma") help first-timers immediately understand the ideal input structure?
